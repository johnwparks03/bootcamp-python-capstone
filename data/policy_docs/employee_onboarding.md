# Employee Onboarding Policy

**Document ID:** POL-004  
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2023  
**Last Updated:** June 20, 2024

## 1. Purpose

The onboarding process is designed to help new employees succeed by providing clear expectations, necessary resources, and mentorship during their first 90 days at Demonbreun Goods.

## 2. Pre-Arrival

Before the first day:
- Send welcome email with office location, parking, and arrival instructions
- Ensure workstation is set up (computer, phone, desk supplies)
- Create accounts for email, VPN, and essential systems
- Assign an onboarding buddy from the same team

## 3. Day One

- Welcome meeting with direct manager (30 minutes)
- Facility tour and safety overview
- System access provisioning and security training
- Completion of HR paperwork and benefit elections
- Team introduction and lunch with onboarding buddy

## 4. Week One

- Attendance at company orientation (history, culture, organizational structure)
- Department overview and role-specific training
- Review of role expectations and success metrics
- Access to documentation and training materials
- Introduction to key stakeholders across the organization

## 5. Month One

- Bi-weekly check-ins with manager to discuss progress and questions
- Completion of role-specific onboarding checklist
- Hands-on training and mentorship from onboarding buddy
- Introduction to team processes and tools
- Completion of mandatory compliance training (Anti-harassment, Data Privacy, Security Awareness)

## 6. Months Two and Three

- Monthly one-on-ones with manager focusing on progress against success metrics
- Continued hands-on support and knowledge transfer
- Opportunity to contribute to projects with oversight
- Mid-point feedback (week 6) and end-of-probation feedback (week 12)

## 7. 90-Day Review

- Formal review of performance against role expectations
- Manager recommendation for employment confirmation or probationary extension
- Discussion of career development goals and next steps
- Completion of onboarding checklist

## 8. Continuous Learning

New employees are encouraged to:
- Attend relevant internal training sessions
- Participate in mentorship programs
- Join company social events and affinity groups
- Seek feedback from colleagues and managers regularly

## 9. Accessibility

Onboarding materials are provided in accessible formats. Employees with disabilities should notify HR if accommodations are needed.
