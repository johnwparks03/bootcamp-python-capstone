# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

**Document ID:** POL-006  
**Effective Date:** September 1, 2023  
**Last Updated:** June 15, 2024

## 1. Purpose

Demonbreun Goods embraces flexible work arrangements to support employee wellbeing, productivity, and retention while maintaining strong team collaboration and company culture.

## 2. Eligibility

Remote work eligibility is determined by role and business needs:
- **Fully Remote Eligible**: Most individual contributor roles in product, engineering, design, marketing, and operations
- **Hybrid Eligible**: Roles requiring in-person collaboration (customer success, sales, facilities, finance teams) must be in office at least 2 days per week
- **On-Site Required**: Roles involving hardware, security, or physical infrastructure must be in office full-time

Managers determine eligibility based on role requirements and team needs.

## 3. Approval Process

- Employees requesting remote work must submit a proposal to their manager
- The proposal should include work arrangements, communication plans, and how remote work supports business objectives
- Manager approval is required; final approval by department leadership for new remote arrangements
- Work arrangements should be documented in the employee file and reviewed annually

## 4. Work Environment

Remote employees are responsible for:
- Maintaining a safe, professional, and secure work environment
- Ensuring reliable internet connectivity (minimum 25 Mbps download, 5 Mbps upload)
- Using VPN and security tools as required by IT
- Protecting company data and adhering to security policies
- Limiting distractions and interruptions during work hours

## 5. Communication and Collaboration

- Remote employees must be available during core hours (9 AM - 3 PM local time)
- Synchronous meetings (video preferred) shall be scheduled during core hours
- Asynchronous communication is encouraged for non-urgent matters
- Remote employees are expected to attend in-person team events and all-hands meetings (at least quarterly)

## 6. Equipment and Support

- Company provides laptop and necessary office equipment
- Remote employees may request home office supplies (desk, chair, monitor) up to $500 annually
- Company reimburses reasonable internet, phone, and utilities costs (coordination with finance required)
- IT provides technical support and VPN access

## 7. Hybrid Work Schedule

- Default schedule is 2-3 days in office per week (flexible arrangement with manager)
- In-office days should align with team collaboration and meeting schedules
- Core collaboration days may be established by team (e.g., Tuesdays and Thursdays)
- Vacation days do not require in-office attendance

## 8. Performance and Expectations

Remote work arrangements are evaluated based on:
- Quality and timeliness of work output
- Engagement in team collaboration and communication
- Meeting core hours and availability expectations
- Adherence to company policies and security protocols

Underperformance may result in transition back to in-office work.

## 9. Changes and Termination

- Remote work arrangements may be changed with 30 days notice if business needs shift
- Relocation more than 100 miles from the office or to a different time zone requires manager and legal approval
- Employees transitioning to new roles may be required to change their work arrangement
