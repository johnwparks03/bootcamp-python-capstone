# Customer Complaint Handling and Resolution Policy

**Document ID:** POL-003  
**Effective Date:** May 20, 2023  
**Last Updated:** August 15, 2024

## 1. Objective

Demonbreun Goods is committed to resolving customer complaints fairly, transparently, and promptly. This policy ensures consistent, professional handling of all customer issues.

## 2. Complaint Categories

Complaints are categorized as:

- **Tier 1 (Low)**: Product arrived damaged, minor quality issue, shipping delay <5 days
- **Tier 2 (Medium)**: Significant quality defect, unauthorized charge, major shipping delay (5-15 days)
- **Tier 3 (High)**: Repeated issues, data privacy concern, false advertising claim, refund dispute

## 3. Initial Response

All complaints must receive an initial response within 24 business hours:
- Acknowledge receipt of the complaint
- Assign a case number
- Provide the customer with a direct contact and timeline for resolution

Escalate to Tier 3 if the complaint involves safety, privacy, or legal concerns.

## 4. Investigation and Resolution

**Tier 1**: Resolved by frontline support (refund or replacement) within 5 business days.

**Tier 2**: Investigated by support supervisor; may require product inspection or carrier investigation. Target resolution: 10 business days.

**Tier 3**: Escalated to the Customer Success Director. Legal and Product teams consulted as needed. Target resolution: 15 business days, with weekly updates to the customer.

## 5. Resolution Options

Preferred resolution paths, in order:
1. Fix or replace the product
2. Offer a refund (full or partial as appropriate)
3. Provide account credit or discount on future purchases
4. Offer expedited shipping on next order at no charge

## 6. Documentation

All complaints and resolutions must be logged in the customer complaint tracking system within 24 hours of resolution. Data is reviewed monthly for trends and process improvement.

## 7. Appeals

Customers dissatisfied with a resolution may appeal to the Customer Success Director within 30 days of the original resolution. Appeals are reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

## 8. Refund Policy

- Standard refunds are processed within 5-7 business days of approval
- Refund method matches the original payment method
- Restocking fee of 10% applies to non-defective returns initiated after 30 days
- Defective products receive full refunds regardless of timeframe
