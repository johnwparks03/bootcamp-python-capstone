# Information Security Policy

**Document ID:** POL-001  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2023  
**Last Updated:** September 1, 2024

## 1. Overview

Demonbreun Goods is committed to protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of all information assets. This policy establishes minimum security standards for all employees, contractors, and third parties with access to Demonbreun Goods systems and data.

## 2. Data Classification

All data is classified into four categories:

- **Public**: Information that can be freely shared (marketing materials, press releases)
- **Internal**: Information for internal use only (internal communications, policies)
- **Confidential**: Sensitive business data (financial records, customer lists, pricing strategies)
- **Restricted**: Highly sensitive data subject to legal/regulatory protection (customer payment info, employee SSNs, health records)

## 3. Access Control

- All employees must use strong passwords (minimum 12 characters, mixed case, numbers, symbols)
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for access to any Confidential or Restricted systems
- Access to data shall be granted on a need-to-know basis
- Privileged access requests must be approved by department managers and the Security team
- Access must be reviewed and revoked within 5 business days of role change or termination

## 4. Data Protection

- All Confidential and Restricted data must be encrypted at rest (AES-256 or equivalent)
- All data in transit must use TLS 1.2 or higher
- Customer payment information is never stored; transactions are processed through PCI-compliant third-party providers
- Database backups must be encrypted and stored offsite

## 5. Incident Reporting

Security incidents or suspected breaches must be reported to the Security team immediately and no later than 24 hours after discovery. Incidents involving Restricted data must be reported to legal and relevant regulatory authorities within 72 hours.

## 6. Compliance

This policy complies with ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II, and applicable data protection regulations. Violations may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.
