# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

**Document ID:** POL-005  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2023  
**Last Updated:** September 1, 2024

## 1. Overview

Demonbreun Goods values work-life balance and provides generous paid time off for employees to rest, recharge, and spend time with family and friends.

## 2. PTO Accrual

- **Years 0-2**: 20 days per year (includes vacation, sick leave, and personal days)
- **Years 3-5**: 25 days per year
- **Year 6+**: 30 days per year

PTO accrues monthly. Unused PTO rolls over up to a maximum of 10 days per year (all unused PTO beyond that threshold is forfeited unless the employee is departing the company, in which case they are paid out).

## 3. Holidays

The following days are paid company holidays:
- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Presidents' Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving (2 days)
- Christmas (2 days)

## 4. Taking PTO

- Employees must request PTO at least two weeks in advance for planned vacation
- Sick leave may be taken with as little notice as practicable (notification to manager before work start time when possible)
- PTO requests are approved by the direct manager, considering team coverage and project schedules
- During peak business seasons, vacation requests may be delayed or modified to ensure operational continuity

## 5. Scheduling Requirements

- Maximum of 10 consecutive days off without special approval
- Employees taking extended leave (>10 days) must coordinate with their manager and department leadership
- PTO should be distributed throughout the year; concentrating all PTO in one period requires manager approval

## 6. Sick Leave

PTO includes sick leave for the employee's own medical care or the care of an immediate family member. Employees are expected to notify their manager as soon as possible if sick leave will be extended beyond one day.

## 7. Unpaid Leave

Employees may request unpaid leave for personal or family reasons. Requests are reviewed case-by-case and may be granted if coverage is available and the absence does not negatively impact operations.

## 8. Return to Work

Upon return from PTO, employees are expected to be caught up on email and communications. Managers should prioritize onboarding returnees to any changes that occurred during their absence.

## 9. Departure

- Upon termination, employees are paid out for any accrued but unused PTO (up to the accrual cap)
- PTO does not carry over to new employment
